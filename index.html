HOW TO USE

A simple two-step routine.

For best results, use the oil regularly and wash with Vanmala Herbal Shampoo.

01
STEP 1

Hair Oil

Apply a sufficient amount of Vanmala Herbal Hair Oil to the scalp and hair. Massage gently using your fingertips for 5–10 minutes. Leave it on for at least 1 hour or overnight for better results.

02
STEP 2

Hair Shampoo

Wet your hair thoroughly. Apply Vanmala Herbal Hair Shampoo, massage gently into the scalp, and rinse with clean water. Repeat if required.
